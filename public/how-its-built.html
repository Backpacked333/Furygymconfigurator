S-Series Studio

Use a build on its own

Every figure is its own page under /how-its-built/. Embed one in an iframe for a landing page or a loading screen. With no options it shows just the build on a transparent background.

<iframe src="/how-its-built/studio-single.html?theme=light&site=0&interactive=0"
        title="S-Series Studio being built" style="border:0;width:100%;aspect-ratio:16/10"></iframe>
theme=dark|light|tintDark, white, or white with a light sage tint. Light is the default.
frame=cardThe framed card with title, live readout, legend and steps. Without it you get just the build.
site=0Hide the concrete pad and ruler and crop tight to the unit.
interactive=0No hover scrubbing, clicks or keys: for loading screens and backgrounds.
paper=%23ffffffThe colour solids are filled with. Set it to the background behind the build.
speed=1.5Playback speed, from 0.1 to 4.
still=1Show the finished unit only, with no motion.
