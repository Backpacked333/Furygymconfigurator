## Cross-cutting principles

- [DISCOVERED] Static, backend-free SPA. No accounts, payments, or lead capture are simulated; production integrations are deliberately absent (README "Before commercial launch").
- [DISCOVERED] Honesty contract: only source-backed Fury facts (sizes, heights, starting prices, published option names) are presented as facts. Everything else is labelled concept/inquiry. Prices never scale or sum into a fake total.
- [DISCOVERED] Visual quality is a primary differentiator: three.js r186 WebGPU renderer with WebGL 2 fallback, TSL post pipeline, quality tiers.
